Common Misconception

"Plants only do photosynthesis and never breathe oxygen."

✅ The Scientific Truth:

Plants undergo both photosynthesis AND cellular respiration!

  • During the Day (Light): Photosynthesis runs fast, consuming lots of CO₂ and releasing huge amounts of O₂.
  • During the Night (Darkness): Without sunlight, photosynthesis stops! Plants still need energy to live, so they consume O₂ and release CO₂ through cellular respiration.
Check your thinking: Why did the plant in the dark closet sealed jar wilt and reduce oxygen levels over 24 hours? Because it could only respire, not photosynthesize!

Concept Drill

The "Closed System" Rule

🔒 Definition:

A Closed System is a sealed container where no matter (atoms/gas) can enter or leave. However, radiant energy (sunlight) can pass right through clear glass!

Autotroph (Plant)
Consumes CO₂ ➔ Produces O₂
Heterotroph (Animal)
Consumes O₂ ➔ Produces CO₂
Key Priestley Takeaway: An animal alone suffocates; a plant alone in darkness wilts; but together in sunlight, their gas outputs sustain each other!
