💡 Misconception Busters: Common 7th Grade Traps

❌ Common Misconception:

"Plants get their food and grow heavier by absorbing dirt and soil minerals through their roots."

✅ Scientific Truth: Trees make their physical mass primarily out of THIN AIR! During photosynthesis, plants pull invisible Carbon Dioxide gas (CO₂) out of the atmosphere and fix carbon into solid sugar and structural cellulose.
❌ Common Misconception:

"Sunlight is the plant's food."

✅ Scientific Truth: Sunlight is Radiant Energy, not matter! A plant cannot eat sunlight. Instead, sunlight acts like electricity powering a factory. The actual food made is Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆), which stores chemical energy in its bonds.

The Underground Orchid (Rhizanthella gardneri)

Native to Western Australia, this bizarre plant spends its entire lifecycle underground. Its roots and stems never see the light of day—only the tiny flower emerges slightly above leaf litter.

Critical 7th Grade Thinking Challenge:

Can the underground orchid use radiant energy from the Sun? If not, where does it get its glucose and chemical energy to survive?

🧬 Botanical Secret Revealed: Because it receives ZERO sunlight, it has completely lost its photosynthetic ability and contains no chlorophyll! It is a myco-heterotroph: it parasitizes underground mycorrhizal fungi, which are connected to nearby eucalyptus trees. The eucalyptus tree carries out photosynthesis in the sun, sends glucose to the underground fungus, and the sneaky orchid steals glucose from the fungus!
